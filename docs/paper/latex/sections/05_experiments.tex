\section{NUMERICAL SIMULATIONS}\label{sec:experiments}

This section evaluates ECC and RWG on time-limited capture of the tumbling target. After the setup, it reports the training process (Section~\ref{sec:learning-curves}), follows two test tasks through the closed loop (Section~\ref{sec:cases}), compares ECC with learning-guided MPC and with MPC on the full test set (Section~\ref{sec:main}), and examines the components of RWG and the behavior of the coordinated closed loop (Section~\ref{sec:rwg-results}).

\subsection{Simulation Setup}\label{sec:setup}

\textit{1) Scenario:} The scenario, constraint, and task parameters are listed in Table~\ref{tab:scenario}. The target flies on a $500$-km circular orbit with an inclination of $45^{\circ}$, and both spacecraft are propagated with \eqref{eq:kin}--\eqref{eq:fg} by an adaptive Runge--Kutta integrator. The target is a homogeneous box with a grapple rod leading to the port and tumbles freely at a spin rate between $2.1$ and $2.7^{\circ}$/s about an axis that deviates by $10$--$20^{\circ}$ from its body $z$-axis, so that $\Ts\in[133, 171]$~s. Its initial attitude is uniformly distributed on $\SO$. The chaser starts in a uniformly random direction at a range of $12$--$16$~m, with a relative speed of at most $0.05$~m/s, a boresight within $15^{\circ}$ of the port, and per-axis angular rates of at most $0.005$~rad/s. Each task draws its deadline from $\mathcal{U}(140,200)$~s, so that the latest admissible entry time $\te$ lies between $95$ and $155$~s and is comparable to one spin period.

\textit{2) Compared methods:} Table~\ref{tab:methods} lists the compared methods. SAC--MPC executes the proposed intent directly and differs from ECC only in the coordination; it is the main baseline. TD3--MPC replaces SAC by TD3 \cite{fujimoto2018td3}. All learning methods share the execution layer, the observation \eqref{eq:obs}, the action space, the networks, the shaped reward \eqref{eq:shaping}, and the task distribution, and each is trained from scratch with three random seeds for $6000$ decisions under the parameters of Table~\ref{tab:params}. MPC applies the near-field MPC of Section~\ref{sec:executor} from the initial instant, with its reference at the capture point rotating with the target and without the decision and stage layers; it represents constrained short-horizon optimization without long-horizon decisions.

\textit{3) Evaluation:} The test set consists of $192$ initial conditions that do not occur in training. Each learning method is evaluated with the final model of each seed and the mean action, giving $576$ test tasks, and MPC is evaluated once on the $192$ initial conditions. All methods are evaluated on the same initial conditions, so every comparison is paired; $95\%$ confidence intervals of paired differences are obtained by a bootstrap that resamples the initial conditions within each seed ($10\,000$ resamples). The metrics are the capture rate and the causes of failure, the completion time and the velocity increment on the tasks captured by both methods of a pair, the return $G$ of the unshaped reward \eqref{eq:reward}, and the computation time of the coordination; capture-rate differences are given in percentage points (pp).
